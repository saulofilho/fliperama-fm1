FLIPERAMA

The music plays the game.

A neighbourhood arcade inside the FM-1. Every cabinet is a sound chip with its own pixel game, and every note moves it: a jump, a shot at goal, a firework. You write the beat. The game plays itself.

01 // The arcade

Six cabinets

With no coin, each cabinet runs its demo in silence, following its own music. Drop a coin to hear the chip in your browser and watch the scene follow every note. Then play along on the keys A to L.

02 // Signals

The screen listens

Each game reads the same signals the chip plays: the note, its pitch, the accent, the envelope, the LFO and the sequencer step.
